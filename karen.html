The Karen Story

A Little Piece of Eritrea in Karen's Greenery

Karen has always been Nairobi's most serene corner — a suburb of wide roads, lush gardens, and a pace of life that feels a world away from the city's centre. When Asmara chose Karen for one of its flagship branches, it was a perfect match: a restaurant built on warmth and cultural heritage, finding a home in a neighbourhood that prized exactly those qualities.

"In Karen, we didn't just open a restaurant. We planted a garden where cultures meet, children play, and families remember what a Sunday afternoon is supposed to feel like."

Located at 321 Ngong Road, the Karen branch is widely regarded as the most picturesque of all Asmara locations. Its expansive lawn, mature trees, and dedicated outdoor play area — complete with swings, slides, and tunnels — have made it the unrivalled choice for Karen families and weekend outings.

The branch also earned accolades at the Chef's Delight Awards for Best Eritrean/Ethiopian Cuisine — a testament to the uncompromising kitchen standards maintained across every Asmara location. Whether it's a lazy Sunday brunch, a romantic dinner under the trees, or a children's birthday celebration on the lawn, Karen delivers an experience that feels genuinely special.
