The Lavington Story

Where Othaya Road Found Its Flavour

When Asmara Restaurant first opened its doors in Pangani in 2009, bringing the warmth of Eritrean hospitality to Nairobi, few could have predicted just how deeply the brand would embed itself into the city's dining culture. The Lavington branch arrived as a natural next step — a neighbourhood craving authentic, soul-warming food didn't have to travel far anymore.

"We wanted Lavington families to feel at home the moment they walked through the door — the same way our grandmothers welcomed everyone in Asmara."

Located on Othaya Road in the heart of Lavington, this branch was designed with the neighbourhood in mind. Families with young children, professionals working from the nearby offices, and the weekend lunch crowd all found a comfortable corner here. The kids' play area — complete with a trampoline and bouncing castle — became legendary among Nairobi parents, who could finally savour a meal uninterrupted while their children played safely in sight.

Today, the Lavington branch remains one of the most beloved in the Asmara family, known for its impeccable service, generous portions, and the unmistakable aroma of injera that drifts through the open windows every morning.
