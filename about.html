Est. 1970s

The Legacy of the Lake

The Founder: Mary Auma Oliech, known as Asmara Hotel, was the mother of legendary Kenyan footballer Dennis Oliech. She established the business to provide for her children.Tragic Loss: The family matriarch passed away after a long battle with cancer in July 2018.Current Operations: Following her passing, the restaurant has continued to be run by the Oliech family, specifically her daughter Lilian Oliech.Why It's FamousThe restaurant is celebrated for authentic, traditional Kenyan dishes, particularly its signature whole deep-fried lake tilapia served with wet fry, millet or cornmeal ugali, and fresh local vegetables.It has become a staple "must-visit" dining spot in the city for locals.

Roots from the Lake

Our story is inextricably linked to the waters of Lake Victoria. Every morning, before the city stirs, our trusted network of fishermen harvests the finest Tilapia. This isn't just sourcing; it's a sacred trust passed down through generations.

"The taste of the lake must reach the plate as if the water is still whispering to the fish."
